Behind the episodes

How a microdrama gets made

01/08
Hook & logline
  1. 01

    Hook & logline

    Every episode starts from a one-line premise and a 3-second hook — the moment that stops the thumb.

    Logline · Hook
  2. 02

    Script & beats

    A 60–90 second script broken into beats: setup, twist, escalation and a cliffhanger ending.

    ChatGPT · Claude
  3. 03

    Characters & casting

    Each character gets a reference sheet — face, wardrobe and look locked so they stay the same in every shot.

    Midjourney · Nano Banana
  4. 04

    Shot list & storyboard

    Scenes planned for vertical 9:16 — framing, camera moves, blocking and eyelines for every shot.

    Storyboard · 9:16
  5. 05

    Generate the scenes

    Stills become motion shot by shot, with continuity checks so light, costume and space always match.

    Seedance · Kling · Veo
  6. 06

    Voices & sound

    Dialogue voices, sound effects and score that give each scene its tension and mood.

    ElevenLabs · Suno
  7. 07

    Edit, captions & grade

    Cut for pace, burned-in subtitles for sound-off viewing and one consistent colour grade.

    Premiere Pro · After Effects
  8. 08

    Cliffhanger & release

    Each episode ends on a hook for the next, exported for Reels, Shorts and drama apps.

    Reels · Shorts · Apps
